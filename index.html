Learning in public, one commit at a time

I'm a second-year CS student from Dharan, Nepal. I started with curiosity about how websites work and turned it into a disciplined habit: building something small every single week.

My goal is simple — become a job-ready full-stack developer by graduation. That means mastering the frontend craft, understanding backend systems deeply, and learning to ship: deploy, monitor, and improve real products that real people use.
